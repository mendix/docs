## Introduction

The activities described in this document are in the **Testing activities** section of the **Toolbox**. This section is only available when you edit a [testflow](/refguide/testflows/).

The following are the testing activities you can use in your testflow:

* [Assert](/refguide/assert/) – checks whether a condition is true, and stops and fails the testflow if it is not
