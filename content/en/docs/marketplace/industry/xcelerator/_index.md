## Introduction

Siemens Xcelerator is a digital business platform that brings together software, IoT hardware, and digital services from Siemens and certified third-party providers. It covers five core domains — industry, buildings, electrification, grids, and transportation — and enables companies of any size to find, try, and buy the technologies they need to digitalize their operations.

The information in this chapter describes how to use Mendix to adapt and extend applications built on Siemens Xcelerator, allowing you to tailor solutions to your specific business needs.
