## October 2026

### October 21, 2026

#### New Features

* We have introduced the **Mx11** certification add-on, which is displayed on the **Certifications and add-ons** card on the **Overview** tab of your Mendix profile. The add-on shows that you are up to date with the Studio Pro 11 product capabilities. For details, refer to [Mendix Profile](/portal/mendix-profile/#certifications-and-add-ons).
