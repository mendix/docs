## Introduction

Documents in this section provide more information about configuring secret management for Private Mendix Platform. Some of the settings that you configure here are initially set by the [Private Platform Configuration Wizard](/private-mendix-platform/configuration-wizard/). Administrators can also update them at any time after the initial configuration.

For more information about all configuration options available to Private Mendix Platform administrators, refer to [Private Mendix Platform Functionalities - Administrators](/private-mendix-platform/reference-guide/admin/).
