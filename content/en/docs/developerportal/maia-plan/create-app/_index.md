## Introduction

Follow the steps in the following pages to create an app with Maia Spec:

1. [Add project context](/developerportal/maia-spec/project-context/). Providing context is the foundation for Maia to build your project plan. Give Maia clear goals, user details, and requirements to generate an accurate app plan.
2. [Define the scope](/developerportal/maia-spec/define-scope/). Work with Maia to refine and detail the project scope, including the goal, success criteria, target users, and requirements.
3. [Generate the plan](/developerportal/maia-spec/generate-spec/). Maia translates your defined scope into a concrete spec, which includes a series of epics and stories.
4. [Start the development](/developerportal/maia-spec/start-development/). Organize and start working on user stories.

## Plans Created in Maia Plan

If you have plans that were created before the switch from Maia Plan to Maia Spec, they are preserved, but you are prompted to create specs for them. This, however, is only possible if those plans have not already been pushed to Jira or to the Mendix planning tool. 

To convert those plans to specs, follow these steps:

1. Click **Create Specs** in the info banner or in the Maia Chat window. You are shown a **Replace with Specs** confirmation window, which lets you know that your epics and user stories will be replaced with new specs. 
2. Click **Replace with Specs** to continue.
