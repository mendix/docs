## Introduction

A bar chart shows one or more series as horizontal bars.

For the properties that all series-based charts share, such as the **Series** list, **Dimensions**, and **Advanced** settings, see [Common Chart Properties](/appstore/widgets/charts/#common-properties). This page describes the properties that are specific to the bar chart.

## General Section

### Bar Format

This setting controls the bar format:

* **Grouped** – Bars of different series are placed one below the other.
* **Stacked** – Bars of different series are stacked on top of one another, forming one bar.

## Edit Series Dialog Box – Appearance Tab

* **Bar color** – The color of the bars for this series.
